Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of personal information held by GMM Publisher, email gefoldi@gmail.com with the subject “GMM Publisher data deletion”.

Describe the information you want removed and provide enough information to identify the relevant record. Do not send passwords or access tokens. We may request proportionate identity verification before acting on your request.

GMM will review the request, remove applicable information under its control unless retention is legally required, and respond in accordance with applicable law. To revoke an app's access to your Facebook account, review your Facebook settings for Apps and Websites or Business Integrations, as applicable. Revoking access does not itself delete all previously stored information.

For information held by Meta itself, use Facebook's own privacy and deletion controls. See our Privacy Policy.
